# Credits: the real-footage corpus

Eight stock clips used to measure the pipeline on real footage (`docs/corpus-baseline.md`). None of the video files is in the repository: `python scripts/fetch_clips.py` downloads each one from the link below, checks its SHA-256 and writes `data/samples/corpus/<name>/clip.mp4` (gitignored). What is committed for each clip is its `ground-truth.json`, written by hand from one frame per second, never from YOLO, and its `roi.json`.

All photos and videos on Pexels are free to use, and they may be modified; attribution is not required. It is not allowed to sell unaltered copies, or to redistribute the files on other stock or wallpaper platforms. That is why the clips are downloaded, not committed. No clip is under a non-commercial or no-derivatives license. Attribution is not legally required; it is given here so the sources can be checked.

**Changes.** Only the video stream is kept (the audio track is dropped). Two clips are cut, re-encoded with libx264 at CRF 18 so that the cut is frame-accurate; the others are copied as they are.
